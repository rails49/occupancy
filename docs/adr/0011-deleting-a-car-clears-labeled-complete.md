# Deleting a car clears labeled complete

Deleting a car clears its image's `labeled_complete` in the same history entry. The manifest cannot tell a label deleted because it covered nothing from one deleted off a car still in the photograph. Keeping the flag in the second case exports an image that teaches the detector that cars are background, and nothing downstream notices. Clearing it in the first case costs one click.

Only a person sets the flag. No default, conversion or accept-all of proposals may. Decided in [#36](https://github.com/rails49/occupancy/issues/36).
