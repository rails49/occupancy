Track trace prototype throwaway

Can a line through clicks be edited locally, extended at both ends, give the same line in either order, and turn exactly straight where the track is straight? Each free click is on the line; its direction comes from its neighbours, and each gap is two arcs. Gaps marked straight or one radius are fitted as one piece, within the tolerance of their clicks.

Walkthroughs

    How to use

    • Click empty space: add a click at the chosen end.
    • Click on the line: insert a click there. Hold and drag to place it.
    • Drag a click: move it. The yellow band shows what changed.
    • Click a click: select it. Shift-click another: select the clicks between.
    • S, R, F: mark the selected gaps, or the gap under the pointer, straight, one radius or free.
    • Delete: remove the selected clicks, or the click under the pointer.
    • Scroll or right-drag or space-drag: pan. Pinch, Ctrl-scroll or + and −: zoom.
    • Cmd-Z, Shift-Cmd-Z: undo, redo. Esc: clear the selection.
